Interpretation

Triton is fastest on most small-M and irregular-N cases in this run. ATen wins two shapes, including the largest square GEMM.

FlyDSL now passes every tested shape with shape-dynamic JIT cache reuse. Its latest wins are on the 256x4096x4096, 2048x4096x4096, and 16x28672x4096 cases; its best throughput is 1135.12 TFLOP/s on 4096x4096x4096.
